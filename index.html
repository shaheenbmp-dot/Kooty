Daily essentials

Welcome

First screen: what Kooty does, language choice, one clear way in.

  • Jakob's law: familiar sign-up pattern
  • Hick's law: one clear choice, Get started
  • Aesthetic-usability effect: calm cream and brand green build trust

Today

The daily home screen: what needs attention now.

  • Von Restorff effect: the gas alert is the only amber card
  • Miller's law: 'This week' shows 4 items
  • Fitts's law: tabs and main buttons in the thumb zone
  • One native ad, clearly marked Sponsored

Reminder calendar

One family calendar. Filter by category, tap a day to see its reminders.

  • Jakob's law: a standard month grid
  • Law of similarity: each category has one colour and one icon
  • Law of proximity: the agenda sits under the grid

Gas tracker

Predicts when gas runs out and books the refill automatically.

  • Tesler's law: the app absorbs the complexity
  • Goal-gradient effect: level and days left are visible
  • User control: auto-book is one clear switch

AI booking call

Live status while the AI assistant books the refill. Steps advance on their own.

  • Doherty threshold: every step shows live
  • Zeigarnik effect: done and remaining steps are clear
  • User control and freedom: take over or end the call

Refill booked

Confirmation and what happens next.

  • Peak-end rule: the flow ends on a clear, happy confirmation
  • Visibility of system status: delivery day and OTP spelled out

Services, home and groups

Services

Find licence-checked electricians, plumbers and more. Categories and filters work.

  • Hick's law: 8 categories, then filter chips
  • Selective attention: Licence verified stands out
  • Law of similarity: every pro card has the same layout

Pro profile

Trust facts, price guide, reviews and a pinned booking bar.

  • Fitts's law: Book is a full-width pinned button
  • Serial position: trust first, price next, reviews last
  • Social proof: rating shown with job count

Home

Shared grocery list, chores and bills for the family. Tick items to try it.

  • Zeigarnik effect: unticked items stay visible
  • Fitts's law: big I'm at the store button
  • Contextual ad: only a deal on something already on the list

Groups

Turf game RSVP, fee split and a food vote.

  • Goal-gradient effect: the 7 of 10 bar
  • Hick's law: RSVP is In, Maybe or Out
  • Peak-end rule: a fun food vote to end the day

Vault

Warranties, documents and receipts, with expiry reminders.

  • Von Restorff effect: Expiring soon stands out
  • Recognition over recall: each item shows time left
  • No ads in the Vault: documents need trust

Design basics

Headings use Baloo 2, body text uses Nunito. Every tap target is at least 44 px and text contrast is at least 4.5:1.
